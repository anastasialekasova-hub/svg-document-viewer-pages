SVG Document Viewer

SVG Document Viewer is installed.

You’re all set.

1. Open your SVG in Chrome

Already open? Stay in that tab. Otherwise, open a direct SVG link, or press Ctrl+O on Windows or ⌘O on Mac and choose your SVG in Chrome.

Don’t have an SVG? Open a sample in a new tab: floor plan, landscape badge or conveyor drawing. In that tab, click the SVG Document Viewer icon and choose Show viewer controls.

2. Click the icon, then Show viewer controls

In the SVG tab, click the SVG Document Viewer icon in Chrome’s toolbar. In the popup, choose Show viewer controls.

Can’t find the icon?

Open Chrome’s Extensions menu (the puzzle icon), find SVG Document Viewer, and click its pin. Then click the SVG Document Viewer icon in the toolbar.

No controls on a local SVG?

If your SVG is already open in a file:// tab, allow file access for the viewer controls:

  1. Open chrome://extensions and find SVG Document Viewer.
  2. Choose Details and turn on Allow access to file URLs.
  3. Return to the SVG tab and reload it. Click the extension icon, then Show viewer controls.

You need this only for an SVG opened from your computer, including one you chose with Ctrl+O or ⌘O. A direct HTTP(S) SVG link works without it. If the popup says it could not access the tab, open Local file help there.
